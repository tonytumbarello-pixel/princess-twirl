# Princess Twirl

Play it here, after the one-time step below:

**https://tonytumbarello-pixel.github.io/princess-twirl/**

## Turn the website on (one time)

1. Open [this settings page](https://github.com/tonytumbarello-pixel/princess-twirl/settings/pages).
2. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Set **Branch** to `main` and the folder to `/docs`.
4. Click **Save**.
5. Wait about a minute, then open the play link above.

Nothing to install. It works on a phone or a computer. Tap the big buttons. Arrow keys and Enter work too. Tap the princess and she talks.
