# Princess Twirl

**Play the game:** [https://tonytumbarello-pixel.github.io/princess-twirl/](https://tonytumbarello-pixel.github.io/princess-twirl/)

Open that link. Nothing to install. It works on a phone or a computer.

This is a dress-up game: pick a princess, a dress, shoes, and sparkles, then parade, have a tea party, or dance. Tap the big buttons. Arrow keys and Enter work too. Tap the princess and she talks.
